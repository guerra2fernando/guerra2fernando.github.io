I started in design at Collinson, building web and loyalty experiences for Intel, Lenovo and Mercedes. Then I moved into growth: automated lead generation at Netshow.me, digital acquisition at Banco BS2, and the acquisition engine at Guru that brought in 60,000 users in 60 days.

At Trademap I designed referral and social loops that acquired 1M+ organic users in twelve months with no media budget. At Santander I owned a $20M multi-brand programme across ten business units, a 20-person team and 80+ agency partners. At Bondai I led market-entry growth for a tourism marketplace. In 2020 I co-founded Exio, an AI growth platform, and took it through a successful exit.

Since then I have been building AI-native products: Lenxys, a live emotionally intelligent assistant; Army, a self-evolving agent orchestrator; OpenOutreach, a Bayesian LinkedIn growth engine; LenManag, an AI website fabric; Xtrac, call intelligence for entire support floors; and LenOS, a workspace where humans and agents share the same rooms.

Most recently I led growth, CRM and lifecycle for an iGaming operator, owning acquisition, retention, VIP and a 120+ campaign calendar. I am now open to new projects where growth and product meet. The through-line is the same as it has always been: understand the system, find the leverage point, build the thing that moves it.

I don't separate product from growth. The loop is the product.

03How I work

Four rules for building things that compound.

The principles are simple. The discipline is in applying them when the deadline is real and the data is incomplete.

01

Ship the whole loop

Strategy is only real when it reaches the interface, the invoice and the retention curve. I build across the whole path instead of stopping at the deck.

02

Measure the outcome

If it cannot be tied to activation, revenue or retention, it is decoration. Instrumentation belongs in the first version, not the third.

03

Design is strategy

The interface is where a business model becomes obvious, or impossible. Visual systems and product logic are the same conversation.

04

Automate the repeatable

Humans should do judgment, relationships and taste. Systems should do the rest. Every repeated task is a candidate for an agent.

04Toolbox

The tools change. The loop stays.

I work across the build and the growth side, which means the stack is deliberately broad. These are the tools I reach for most often.
